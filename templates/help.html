Help

Who can see what you post here, and what those choices do not promise.

Who can see a review

Every review carries the audience that was chosen for it when it was written:

  • Public - anyone can read it, on this site and on the other servers this instance talks to.
  • Followers - only the accounts that follow the author. They see it in their feed whether they live here or on another server.
  • Private - only the author. It appears in their own feed and on their own pages; nothing about it is delivered to other members or to other servers.

{% if user.is_authenticated %} Your Profile / Settings page sets the audience new reviews start with. {% else %} Every member's Profile / Settings page sets the audience their new reviews start with. {% endif %} The audience is chosen when a review is written and does not change afterwards. A reply is seen by whoever can see the post it answers.

What "Private" does not mean

Private means hidden from other members and from other servers. It does not mean hidden from the person who runs this instance: the database sits on their machine, and the site's admin tools can read everything stored here, private posts included.

If your post is reported

A member can only report a post they can actually see - a post you keep to yourself cannot be reported by someone who cannot read it. Once a post is reported, it is shown to moderators whatever audience it was written for, so they can read what the report is about and decide what to do. The audience marker travels with it, so a moderator can see the post was not public.
