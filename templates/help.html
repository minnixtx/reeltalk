Getting around

Where do I find things?

Use the menu on the left on a computer or the bar at the bottom on your phone. On your phone, tap More for the rest of the menu. Select the ReelTalk logo at the top to return to your feed.

What’s the difference between Following, Local, and All?

  • Following shows posts from people you follow.
  • Local shows posts from this ReelTalk site that you can see, including posts by people you haven’t followed yet.
  • All also shows posts your site receives from other servers.

Scroll down or select Older to see more. For something new to watch, try Trending Films, Top Reviews, or Popular Genres beside the feed on wider screens.

Finding and tracking films

How do I find a film and save it for later?

Search by title using the box at the top of the page. ReelTalk searches The Movie Database (TMDB), so you can find films nobody here has reviewed yet. Select Add to watchlist in the results or on the film’s page.

How do I log a film I’ve seen?

Open the film and select Mark as watched. Give it a star rating and add a written review if you like. Once you mark a film as watched, it moves from your Watchlist to Watched.

Where can I see my Watchlist and watched films?

{% if user.is_authenticated %} Open My Films {% else %} Open the Films tab on your profile {% endif %} and choose All films, Watchlist, or Watched. Visitors to your profile can also browse your films.

You can also import a TMDB list or export your films as a CSV from Profile / Settings.

Reviews and replies

How do I post or edit a review?

Select Mark as watched on the film’s page, add your rating and review, then choose who can see it before posting. To change the text or rating later, select Edit review on the film’s page. You can also select Delete on your review to remove it.

Who can see my review?

  • Public reviews are open to anyone and can reach other servers.
  • Followers reviews are visible to accounts that follow you.
  • Private reviews are visible only to you among ReelTalk members.

You can set a default audience for new reviews in Profile / Settings. Check the audience before you post, since it’s fixed once the review is published.

How do I react or join the conversation?

Select the clapperboard to applaud a post. Select it again to take back your applause. Use Reply to join the conversation. A reply is visible to the same audience as the post it answers. To mention someone, type @username or use @username@domain for someone on another server.

How do I report a post?

Use the report option under the post, explain what happened, and submit the report.

Making and saving lists

How do I make a film list?

Your Watchlist is for films you want to watch. Use Lists for collections you want to share, like favorite midnight movies. Open {% if user.is_authenticated %} Lists {% else %} Lists {% endif %} and choose New list. Give it a name, add films by title, and use the arrows to put them in order.

How do I save someone else’s list?

Select Save on the list to find it later under Saved Lists. You’ll see the latest version as the creator makes changes.

Following people

How do I find someone to follow?

Open Find People and search by username. If they’re on another server, use their full handle, such as username@example.org. Open their profile and select Follow. The Local feed is also a good place to find people whose reviews you enjoy.

How do I stop seeing someone’s posts?

Select Block on their profile. You can undo it from the same page with Unblock.

Where do I see replies, mentions, and new followers?

Open Notifications (the bell) for replies, mentions, applause, and new followers. Select Mark all read to clear the unread count.

Profile and sign-in

How do I edit my profile?

Open {% if user.is_authenticated %} Profile / Settings {% else %} Profile / Settings {% endif %} to change your display name, bio, profile picture, or default review audience.

What if I didn’t get my verification email?

Check your spam folder. If the email still hasn’t arrived, request another verification code.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password reset page to get a reset link by email.
