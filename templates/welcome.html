Getting started

ReelTalk is a home for tracking and reviewing films. The core loop takes four steps.

1

Find a film

Use the search bar at the top of any page, or the full search page. Results come from TMDB — click one to open its page.

2

Add it to your Watchlist

On a film's page choose "Add to watchlist" — or use the one-click button right on the search results. Films you want to see collect here.

3

Mark it watched

When you've seen a film, mark it watched from its page: pick a star rating (required) and add an optional written review.

4

Follow film fans
