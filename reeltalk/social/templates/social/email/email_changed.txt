Your email address on {{ site_name }} was changed

The email address on the account @{{ localname }} has just been changed.

  Previous address : {{ old_email }}
  New address      : {{ new_email }}
  Changed          : {{ changed_at }}

This message is a notice only. It carries no link, and nothing in it can
undo the change, confirm a new address, or reach any part of your account.
Only the site administrator can change an address.

Changing an address also clears the verified status of the account. Until
the new address has been confirmed through a link sent to it, this account
is treated as unverified.

If you asked the site administrator to correct your address, you have
nothing to do.

If you did not ask for this change, contact the site administrator as
soon as you can. This message is sent to your previous address precisely
so that you find out about a change you did not make — a change like this
moves every future email for the account, including anything used to
recover access to it.

This message deliberately contains no links. It is going to an address
that may no longer be yours, and anything clickable in it would be
something the person who changed your address could act on too.
