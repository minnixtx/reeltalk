Your password on {{ site_name }} was changed

The password on the account @{{ localname }} has just been changed by the
site administrator.

  Account : @{{ localname }}
  Changed : {{ changed_at }}

You did not change it yourself, so you are being told. This message exists
because replacing somebody's password is the easiest way to take an
account over quietly, and you should not have to notice by finding yourself
locked out.

Every session that was signed in on your account has been signed out, on
this device and on every other. Nobody keeps access from before this
message.

This message is a notice only. It carries no link, and nothing in it can
undo the change, set a new password, or reach any part of your account.
Only the site administrator can change a password.

If you asked the site administrator to reset this for you, you have
nothing to do except log in with the password they gave you.

If you did not ask for this, contact the site administrator as soon as
you can. Ask them to change it again, and ask them to look at the account
for anything you did not do — a password change you did not ask for is
worth treating as a bigger problem until it turns out not to be one.

This message deliberately contains no links. It is going to an address
that the person who changed your password may also be able to read, and
anything clickable in it would be something they could act on too.
