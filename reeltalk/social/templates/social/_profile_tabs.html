{% comment %} The tab strip shared by the three surfaces it spans (profile, films, lists): it sits above the body, like every tabbed surface the site has - the strip says where you are, the body below it is what you are looking at. The caller names the active tab: ``with profile_tab="reviews"`` (or "films", "lists"). Reviews is the profile's own body (D1), so its tab points at the profile URL itself - there is no third URL to page. The strip is always shown, never hidden for being empty (D5); the empty state in the body is what an empty list looks like. The Lists tab is hidden when the member has made no lists - R138 decision 3 - rather than shown with an empty state, because a tab that leads nowhere is worse than one that appears when there is something behind it. ``list_count`` is the whole decision and the view makes it via ``_profile_head_context``; the template never reaches for the database. The one exception is the lists page itself: a strip that hides the tab you are standing on would say you are nowhere, and on your own lists page the tab plainly leads somewhere - it just brought you there. {% endcomment %}
